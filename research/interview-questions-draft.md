# Interview questions: draft v0 (experienced job seeker + networker)

Status: draft for Aryan's review, 2026-10-23. Nothing built. Build plan comes after his edits.

## Persona for this draft
An experienced professional (3+ years) who wants a new job and also wants to meet people in their field.

## Design rules and where they come from
1. **One question per screen, with a suggested answer to react to.** People correct a guess faster than they write from scratch. (`interview-me` skill, addyosmani/agent-skills)
2. **Ladder vague answers two or three levels** until there's a specific example or number. (`customer-research` skill, 5-why laddering)
3. **OARS:** open questions, affirmations, reflections, summaries. (UNH "Motivational Interviewing: The Basics, OARS"; NIH/PMC article on MI.) Affirmations must be specific, never generic praise: people resent scripted affirmations (Reddit r/therapyabuse thread on OARS).
4. **Stuck ladder:** rephrase, then show examples, then park the question. Skip is only allowed on sensitive questions. (`conversational-ux` skill, owl-listener/designer-skills)
5. **Head start:** import what the person already wrote (pasted profile or LinkedIn data archive), so progress starts above 0% and later questions become "confirm or fix" rather than blank. (`onboarding` skill: endowed progress effect)
6. **End on a high:** show the first draft immediately after the summary. (`onboarding` skill: peak-end rule)
7. **Ask only what feeds an output.** Don't collect data no draft uses (for example, salary).

## Tags
- **Core:** required, no skip. When stuck: examples and a shorter follow-up.
- **Sensitive:** skip allowed, no reason asked. Field stays blank.
- **Enrichment:** optional, can be answered later.

## Flow (target 12–15 minutes)

### Step 0: Head start (about 1 minute)
Paste your headline, About and experience, or upload your LinkedIn data archive, or skip. The app pre-fills what it can and asks you to confirm it.

### A. Where you are now
- **A1 [Core]** "What's your current role, and roughly how many years have you done this kind of work?" Feeds: headline, profile fix, outreach intro. Pre-filled from the import. Chips: 3–5, 5–8, 8–12, 12+ years.
- **A2 [Core]** "What do people at work come to you for?" Feeds: positioning line, headline. If generic ("I'm a team player"): "Give me one example from the last few months."
- **A3 [Core]** "Which field best describes your work?" Chips: Sales, Marketing, Software, Data & Analytics, Finance, Design, Operations, HR & Recruiting, Education, Healthcare, Other. Feeds: examples, peers, content pillars.

### B. What you want
- **B1 [Core]** "What do you want LinkedIn to help with in the next 3 months?" Multi-select: a new job, referrals, meeting people in my field, a mentor, clients. Plus free text.
- **B2 [Core]** "What role are you aiming for next, and at what level?" Guess from A1. If vague ("something better"): "If you got an offer tomorrow, what would the job title say?"
- **B3 [Core]** "Name 3–5 companies, or the kind of company, you'd like to work at." Feeds: referral targets and people to meet. Follow-up: "What draws you to them?"
- **B4 [Core, triggered]** "If you didn't have to justify it to anyone, what would you actually want from your next move?" Asked only when B2 or B3 sounds like a "should" answer.
- **B5 [Sensitive]** "What's making you look now?" Chips: growth, pay, layoff, relocation, manager or team, prefer not to say. Feeds: story framing and tone only.
- **B6 [Core]** "Any must-haves? Location, remote work, notice period." Feeds: filters for targets.
- **B7 [Sensitive]** "Does your current employer know you're looking?" Feeds: whether posts can mention the job search. Defaults to private if skipped.
- **Not asked:** salary. No draft uses it.

### C. Your proof
- **C1 [Core]** "Tell me about one piece of work you're proud of from the last 2–3 years." Ladder: What was the situation? What did you personally do? What changed, and by how much? Repeat for up to three wins (minimum one).
- **C2 [Core, triggered]** When a win has no number: "Roughly how many people, hours or rupees did it touch?" or "What would have happened if you hadn't done it?" Estimates are allowed and labelled as estimates.
- **C3 [Enrichment]** "Has anyone praised your work? A message, review or award." Feeds: proof lines.
- **C4 [Enrichment]** "Anything you can show publicly? A deck, case study, article, code or certificate." Feeds: Featured section checklist.
- **C5 [Sensitive]** "Any gaps or changes in your career you'd like help explaining?" Feeds: profile wording.

### D. People you want to meet
- **D1 [Core]** "Who would you most like to talk to in the next month?" Chips: hiring managers at target companies, people in my target role, alumni, seniors in my field, founders. Free text for names you already have in mind.
- **D2 [Core]** "Where do you already have a way in?" College, past companies, communities, groups. Feeds: the ask ladder (alumni and ex-colleagues are warm).
- **D3 [Core]** "What could you offer someone you reach out to?" A perspective, feedback, a resource, a referral of your own. Follow-up: "What do you know that someone two years behind you doesn't?"
- **D4 [Enrichment]** "How comfortable are you reaching out to strangers?" Scale 1–5, then "What usually stops you?" Chips: don't know what to say, feel like I'm bothering them, fear of being ignored, don't know who to contact. Feeds: coaching tone and message types.

### E. How you sound and how much time you have
- **E1 [Enrichment]** "What could you talk about for an hour without notes?" Feeds: content pillars.
- **E2 [Enrichment]** "Paste 1–3 posts or messages you've written that sound like you." Feeds: voice.
- **E3 [Enrichment]** Tone chips: plain and direct, warm, witty. Language: English, or English with Hinglish.
- **E4 [Core]** "How much time can you give this each week?" Chips: 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, 2+ hours. Feeds: weekly plan size.

### F. Read-back and first win
- **F1** Summary card: who you are, what you want (role and companies), your proof (top wins), who to meet, constraints (location, private search), and out of scope (for example, "won't post about the job search"). Buttons: "Looks right" and "Fix a line."
- **F2** First draft right away: a rewritten headline plus one referral message for one target company.

## Behaviour rules
- Every question shows one labelled example. Inserting an example into the box requires editing before it can be saved.
- After each answer, the app reflects it back in one line. Affirmations are specific to what was said, never generic praise.
- Stuck ladder: rephrase, then examples, then park (sensitive questions can be skipped).
- Stop rule: the interview ends when all core fields are filled and confirmed. Unanswered enrichment questions move to a "Later" list.
- Voice or typing, with typing always visible.
- The progress bar starts above zero after the import, counting fields already filled.
